# 难度四档的几条规矩在游戏里的样子。每一档各跑一遍（环境变量 SW_RULESET 临时指定难度，不写存档）：

#   SW_RULESET=hard    tools/playtest/agent.sh a7 drive tools/playtest/steps_rules.txt rules_hard
#   SW_RULESET=extreme tools/playtest/agent.sh a7 drive tools/playtest/steps_rules.txt rules_extreme
# 每一档里查：左上角的旗子和蜡烛露没露面、朝着四个方向各能跳多远、手里的灯放得放不得、蜡烛烧完灯灭不灭、
# 结算面板上"再守这一关"在不在。哪一样和这一档该有的样子对不上，debug_rules_check 就报"没过"

sleep 3

call pad_adapter.gd debug_reach
call pad_adapter.gd debug_deal
call pad_adapter.gd debug_rules_check play
rects
snap 01_level1

call pad_adapter.gd debug_goto_level 5

call pad_adapter.gd debug_rules_check over
